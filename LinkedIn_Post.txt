I am excited to share my Hotel Booking Cancellation Risk Prediction project, a machine learning portfolio project focused on a real business challenge in hospitality: identifying bookings that are more likely to be canceled.

Cancellations can affect revenue forecasting, room inventory planning, staffing, and customer engagement. The goal of this project was not only to build a predictive model, but to translate booking data into practical business insight that hotel teams could use to act earlier.

Project highlights:

- Cleaned dataset: 36,248 hotel bookings
- Cancellation rate: 32.8%
- Best model: Random Forest
- Accuracy: 88%
- ROC-AUC: 0.94
- Final confusion matrix: TN=4,437, FP=436, FN=407, TP=1,970

The workflow included data cleaning, exploratory analysis, feature engineering, preprocessing, class balancing with SMOTE, model comparison, and final business interpretation.

The Random Forest model showed strong performance in ranking cancellation risk, which could help hotels prioritize customer follow-up, improve revenue planning, monitor higher-risk booking channels, and design more targeted retention strategies.

Key business takeaway:
Predictive analytics can help hotels move from reacting to cancellations after they happen to proactively managing cancellation risk before it affects operations and revenue.

This project strengthened my ability to connect machine learning outputs with business decision-making, especially in customer behavior, revenue management, and operational planning.
